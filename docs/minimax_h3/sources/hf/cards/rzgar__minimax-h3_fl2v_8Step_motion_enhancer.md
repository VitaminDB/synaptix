## Model description 

An 8-step LoRA for the **MiniMax-H3 FL2VS** video model, built on top of the vanilla [LightX2V 8-step LoRA](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Minimax-h3-Turbo/blob/main/minimax_h3_fl2v_turbo_8step_v1.0_comfyui_bf16.safetensors).

It focuses on the NSFW side of generation:

- More accurate male and female anatomy
- Understands NSFW-related motions and enhances the animations
