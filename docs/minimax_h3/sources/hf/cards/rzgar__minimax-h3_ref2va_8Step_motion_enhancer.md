## Model description 

An 8-step LoRA for the **MiniMax-H3 ref2va** video model, built on top of the vanilla [LightX2V 8-step LoRA](https:&#x2F;&#x2F;huggingface.co&#x2F;lightx2v&#x2F;Minimax-h3-Turbo&#x2F;blob&#x2F;main&#x2F;minimax_h3_ref2v_turbo_8step_v1.0_768p_bf16.safetensors).

It focuses on the NSFW side of generation:

- More accurate male and female anatomy
- Understands NSFW-related motions and enhances the animations
