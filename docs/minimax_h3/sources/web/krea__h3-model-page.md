Frequently Asked Questions

What is MiniMax H3?

MiniMax H3 is the frontier video generation model from MiniMax and the successor to Hailuo 2.3. It renders at 2K, generates clips from 5 to 15 seconds, and conditions on tagged image, video, and
audio references. The Hailuo family is known for dynamic, physics-aware motion and expressive character performance — including facial micro-expressions — in AI-generated video.

Is MiniMax H3 the same as Hailuo 3?

Yes. MiniMax H3 is the official name; Hailuo 3, Hailuo 3.0, and Hailuo H3 all refer to the same frontier Hailuo video model from MiniMax. On Krea it is listed as MiniMax H3 in the video model
selector.

Is MiniMax H3 available on Krea?

Yes. MiniMax H3 is live on Krea — open the Krea Video Tool, pick MiniMax H3 from the model selector, and generate. Hailuo 2.3 Fast is still in the selector. Hailuo 2.3 is not.

When is the MiniMax H3 release date?

MiniMax H3 has been released and is available on Krea now. You can generate with it in the Krea Video Tool without waiting for a rollout.

Does MiniMax H3 have 2.7 trillion parameters?

No. The 2.7-trillion-parameter model in July 2026 reporting is M3 Pro, a large language model MiniMax plans to open-source — not a video model. MiniMax has not published a parameter count for MiniMax
H3.

What is new in MiniMax H3 compared to Hailuo 2.3?

MiniMax H3 renders at 2K, extends clip length to 15 seconds, and adds tagged references — up to 9 images, 3 videos, and 3 audio clips in a single generation — alongside start and end frame animation.
Hailuo 2.3 is not in the Krea Video selector. Hailuo 2.3 Fast still is.

Does MiniMax H3 support image-to-video?

Yes. Add a start image to guide the opening frame, or set both a start and an end frame to animate between them and lock how the shot begins and ends.

Does MiniMax H3 generate audio?

No. MiniMax H3 generates video, and accepts audio clips as tagged references to condition a generation. For video with generated sound, use a model with native audio output such as LTX-2.3 or Veo 3.1
on Krea.

How do I use MiniMax H3 on Krea?

Open the Krea Video Tool, choose MiniMax H3 from the model selector, add your prompt or start image, and generate — the same workflow you use for other video models on Krea.

Can I use MiniMax H3 videos for commercial work?

Yes. On Krea paid plans, generated videos include commercial licensing for campaigns, products, client work, and other production projects.

Can I compare MiniMax H3 with other video models?

Yes. Krea lets you switch between video models like Kling, Veo, Sora, and Seedance in the same workflow, so you can run the same prompt through MiniMax H3 and the others and compare motion, prompt
adherence, and output quality.

Who is MiniMax H3 for?

MiniMax H3 is aimed at filmmakers, marketers, designers, and creative teams that want cinematic AI video with realistic motion for ads, social content, storyboards, and production iteration.
