What to Test Beyond the Demo Reel

Reference direction

On PixVerse, H3 Reference mode accepts text with image, video, and audio references. That matters when the brief separates identity, action, camera movement, edit rhythm, and sound into different
assets. Give each reference one job, then see whether the result keeps those jobs separate instead of taking the mood from one image and ignoring the rest. Audio cannot be the only reference input,
so pair it with an image or video when it carries the sound direction.

For teams building those reference packs, the PixVerse Canvas workflow keeps approved stills, motion clips, and prompt versions in one place.

Motion, people, and props

MiniMax presents H3 as a model for more coherent action and physical behavior. The checks are not glamorous: do hands stay on the prop, does a jacket keep its shape when the camera moves, and does
the room stay put when two people cross in front of each other? Those details decide whether a beautiful opening frame survives an edit.

If identity is the main risk, start with a consistent character pack and keep the identity instructions fixed across every attempt. Our guide to consistent AI video characters explains the inputs
worth locking before changing action or camera direction.

Audio and local edits

The manual also documents native audio, lip sync, voice-timbre transfer, and edits to characters, objects, backgrounds, visual effects, and dialogue. Test those jobs separately. A clip can have good
room tone and miss the dialogue timing; an object swap can look fine until a hand passes in front of it. Name what must stay untouched as clearly as the change you want.

Before You Put H3 in a Production Workflow

  • Check the single estimated credit total before you generate; it combines output and eligible reference inputs, with Ultra shown after its member discount.
  • Test one interaction-heavy scene with hands, props, and a moving camera before committing the model to a larger production.
  • Keep source-footage, voice, likeness, music, logo, and commercial-use rights in the project brief rather than treating them as an afterthought.
  • Save the good runs and the bad ones. One striking generation is not enough to choose a model.

FAQ

What is MiniMax H3?

MiniMax H3 is a multimodal AI video workflow that combines text, images, video, and audio. On PixVerse, it creates 5-15 second 2K clips through Video, Transition, and Reference modes.

Are the six videos independently tested?

No. They are official MiniMax demonstrations selected to show different creative directions. They are not evidence of repeatable results, current availability, or production reliability.

What should teams test first in MiniMax H3?

Start with the failure that would hurt your project most: product geometry, character identity, hand-and-prop interaction, camera direction, or audio timing. Hold the source assets and prompt
constant across two or more attempts before making a production decision.
